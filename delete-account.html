Shakthi Tech Solutions LLC — ContextKeep

Delete in the app

Open Settings > Delete account and cloud data. If asked to authenticate again, sign out and sign in again, then retry deletion.

This removes your ContextKeep Firebase Authentication account and cloud-synced entries, and clears local entries on that device.

Request deletion without the app

Email shakthitechsolutionsllc@gmail.com with the subject ContextKeep account deletion, preferably from the Google email you use to sign in to ContextKeep. We may ask you to verify account ownership before processing your request. Never send your password, payment information, or the contents of your notes.

Other copies and subscriptions

Clear ContextKeep app storage on your other devices to remove cached copies. Exports, backups, and copies you previously shared must be removed separately from their destinations.

Account deletion does not cancel a paid subscription. Cancel ContextKeep Pro in Google Play > Payments & subscriptions > Subscriptions.
